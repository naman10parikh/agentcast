# BRAND — agentcast

## Name Rationale

**agentcast** — agents that are *cast* (like casting actors for roles): reusable, composable personas
dropped into any production. The "cast" metaphor signals that these are defined characters with
specific capabilities, not generic workers.

## Tagline

> "Cast the right agent for every role."

## Position

agentcast sits between the harness (Energy meta-system) and the model (Claude). It owns the
**sub-agents** slot in the harness anatomy: curated persona library + flat-path standard + validator.

## Colors

- Primary: `#0ea5e9` (sky-500) — clear, precise, professional
- Accent: `#6366f1` (indigo-500) — depth, intelligence
- Background: `#0f172a` (slate-950) warm-dark
- Text: `#f1f5f9` (slate-100)

## Related Projects

- **armory** — catalog of all tools (agentcast personas are a subset)
- **agentdial** — dial up/down model settings per task
- **agentgrid** — orchestration layer that consumes agentcast personas
